GBA Studio's Pokémon FireRed profile is a focused Python adaptation of the upstream
Gen III ROM-reading, LZ77, 4bpp tile, palette, and pointer-table extraction approach.
The upstream repository is archived. The profile in this application currently
supports Pokémon FireRed USA (BPRE) species front/back/shiny sprites and trainer
front sprites only. It is not a full port of the upstream extractor.
